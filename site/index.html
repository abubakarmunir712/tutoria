What it does

Help with homework, whenever it is needed

Tutoria explains ideas the way a patient teacher would, then checks that they have stuck.

💬

Ask Tutoria

Ask anything about a subject and get a clear, step-by-step explanation. Past conversations are saved, so a lesson can be picked up again later.

🎙️

Voice Tutor

Talk it through out loud and hear the answer back. Voice works in English.

📷

Snap and Solve

Take a photo of a homework question and Tutoria walks through how to solve it.

📚

Learn by topic

Browse subjects and topics built around Ghana's NaCCA curriculum for the learner's class.

✅

Quizzes and practice

Short quizzes, quick checks and assignments for each unit. Tutoria gives a nudge on a wrong answer, then shows how to get there.

🏆

XP, streaks and badges

Learning earns points, daily streaks and badges, with a weekly class leaderboard that can be switched off.

For parents and guardians

You stay in the picture

  • One parent account for each child, each with their own class and subjects.
  • See your child's progress, streak and how well they know each topic.
  • Add more than one child to the same account and switch between them.
  • No unrelated advertising to children, and we do not sell personal information.

A helper, not a replacement

Tutoria uses AI, and AI can make mistakes. It is meant to support what children learn at school, not to replace teachers or parents.
